Embers of the Empire

The Lothlian Empire collapsed in a week. Now, on the burning frontier of the Reaves, three battered souls are drawn toward the war that will decide what rises from its ashes.

A ruthless horse lord fighting to preserve his people, a sword-maiden trapped between duty and freedom, and a disgraced imperial knight searching for one last chance at redemption. Warring clans, northern raiders, and the monstrous Wyrm Lords. Survival will demand a terrible price.

About Logan

Logan has travelled to more than 50 countries for work and pleasure. He lifted in Arnold’s childhood gym in Austria, asked his wife to marry him in an abandoned castle in Ireland, bartered for jewellery in a Kuwaiti souk, sang German songs quite poorly at Oktoberfest, and burned a Viking ship at Hogmanay in Edinburgh.

George R.R. Martin, Steven Pressfield, Bernard Cornwell and Robert Jordan fill his shelves and shape his books. He lives in Virginia, which has enough history to keep him busy until the end of time, with his wife, children, and a dog named Ronin the Barbarian. When there is free time, he throws axes, usually at targets.
