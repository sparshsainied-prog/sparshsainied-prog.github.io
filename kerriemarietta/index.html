Her debut novel

This Too

When small-town girl Abigail moves to Melbourne for university, she hopes it’s her chance to shed her “sweet and naïve” label and finally become like her best friend, Luna: bold, magnetic, and effortlessly confident. But beneath her seemingly perfect facade, Luna hides the scars of her mother’s abandonment and her father’s suffocating control, concealing her true self even from Abigail.

As new relationships and old wounds collide, the two friends’ bond begins to strain under the weight of secrets, jealousy, and unspoken truths.
