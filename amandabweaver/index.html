Cast down

Sapphira Gail is dead, cast down to the worst part of Pandemonia by a terrifying, mercurial goddess who determines the fate of every being.

She plans to escape, confront the goddess and return across the veil to the land of the living, to find the person responsible for her death. To get there she must travel the Path of the Damned through every part of this hellscape.
