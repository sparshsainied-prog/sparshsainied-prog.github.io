Bella puts the story back in history

Children love stories. So why do so many dislike history? Maybe because they haven’t met Bella yet.

Bella is a golden retriever with a nose for history and an ear for storytelling. On her Texas farm she tells her three puppies, Freddy, Abby and Emma, true tales of the American Revolution and the founding of the USA, just in time for America’s 250th birthday.
