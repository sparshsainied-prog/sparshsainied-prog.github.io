Charlie Gold is thirteen, six foot two and more mathlete than athlete.

One brawl, one viral video. So he starts crunching the numbers.

Let the games begin.

Brave New Game

Charlie wants to be a basketball star like his late father. When that crashes, his number-crunching might get an underdog college team, and him, back on track.

For the kids who love the game from the bench

I’ve been a teacher and school librarian for 16 years, and sports books are how a lot of the boys in my classroom started reading.

Charlie uses calculus to tutor the team’s star player and statistics to scout recruits. He’s lovable, and he also makes poor decisions, lies and cheats. Teens make mistakes.
