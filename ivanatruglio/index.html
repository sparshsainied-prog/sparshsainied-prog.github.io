Mercantili

Cosy fantasy. Carlotta Mercantessa and her Guild.

Carlotta is ambitious, proud and scheming, and she is on her way to becoming the youngest ever Maestra of the Mercantili Guild. Then a tragic accident leaves her companion Filippo with a devastating injury, and what she finds beneath her Guild's glittering facade is worse.

"Desperate to save the Mercantili, just how far will she go to secure their future? And who will pay the price?"

In her words

Ivana L. Truglio

She calls herself a book lover, and says she always has been. She made up her mind to be a writer when she was six years old and never looked back.

Along the way she learned to fly, studied archaeology and ancient history, and worked in office jobs. For 11 years she worked for multinational publishing companies in the tax team, and learned the tricks of the trade to start her own publishing company.
