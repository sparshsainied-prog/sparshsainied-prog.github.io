The campus

From the snowy streets of London to the aurora-lit halls of North Pole University, partnership magic, the rare soul-deep bond between perfectly matched individuals, is reshaping the supernatural world.

Enchanted campus. Shifter soulmates. Each book follows its own couple.

Marie-Hélène

I was a special education teacher before I became a full-time author. I live in Sherbrooke, Quebec, and I like hiking, cycling and a book on the beach. I recently discovered I was neurodivergent, so you’ll find my stories are very inclusive.

I’m a member of the Quebec Writers’ Federation (QWF) and UNEQ, Quebec’s writers’ union.
