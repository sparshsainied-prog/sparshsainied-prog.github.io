It really happened.

In the days after Christmas Prelude, someone left a tree by the road in coastal Maine. Instead of letting it go to the trash, we carried it down to the beach, anchored it in the cold sand and wrapped it in solar lights.

People stopped to admire it and, without a word, began adding offerings of their own. One day, painted rocks appeared beneath the branches, just like wrapped presents. To this day, no one knows who left them.

A nor'easter swept the tree out to sea. Weeks later, a familiar twinkle blinked in the seagrass. Every year since, a tree returns to that same stretch of beach, shared by neighbours and strangers alike.

Donna K.

Donna has spent more than thirty years on the coast of Maine, and she and her family treasure their years in Goose Rocks. The shoreline, the seagrass and the changing seasons are where this story comes from.

Her dog Finn is the real-life inspiration for Hildi's faithful companion. The Magical Beach Tree is her first children's book.
