The Oasis

It is the summer of 1992 in small-town North Carolina. Ten-year-old Charly Harding has had a rough life.

Her parents died when she was five, and she and her little brother Matty moved three hours away to live with Granny. Then their double-wide trailer burns to the ground, and the three of them move into a dingy motel called The Oasis, which ain’t no oasis. With her birthday looming, Charly tries to stay positive and thankful, and leans on close friends for help.

Hi, I’m Lorisa.

I write heartfelt, hopeful stories about finding strength through life’s challenges.

I grew up in West Virginia. I have a B.A. in Special Education and an M.Ed. as a Reading Specialist, and my background is working with children and adults with developmental disabilities. When I became a mother I stepped away from that work for a while, and found that my real dream was to be a writer.

My first books were poetry, Love Complications and then Reflections. After years of learning, writing, critiquing and editing, The Oasis came out with Orange Hat Publishing in May 2025, and Charly’s story carries on in Charly’s Volcano.

I live in Wisconsin, and I love my daily matcha, embroidery, long walks, and any chance to be near water.
