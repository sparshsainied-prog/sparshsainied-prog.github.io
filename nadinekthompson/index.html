Between full teaching days and tired evenings, she set herself a challenge: write for thirty days.

It was a way to keep creating, even when inspiration felt far away. Some days the words came less easily, but she kept returning to the page.

What began as a challenge became a deeply personal practice of reflection, release and renewed creative flow.
