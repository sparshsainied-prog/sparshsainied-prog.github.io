New novel

The Princess Thief

Sapphic science fiction. A retelling of The Goose Girl.

Sian is sent to the planet Phoenix Gate to steady its shattered monarchy after an attack wipes out the royal family. En route, her girlfriend steals her identity and leaves her stranded. Now she is a goose keeper that no one believes, and the only person who might help is the knight investigating the conspiracy.

"Sword fighting, slow-burn sapphic romance, political intrigue and too many geese."

On the cover: "It's only theft if it wasn't already yours."
