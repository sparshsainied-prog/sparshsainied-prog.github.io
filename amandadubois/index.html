I don't research before I start a story.

My stories come from actual legal situations that my clients or friends have faced. My courtroom scenes are vivid because I have spent countless hours in those cold and unfriendly spaces.

My hospital scenes come directly from my experience as a registered nurse. The rest of my stories come from being a lifelong listener.

Amanda

Founder and managing partner of DuBois Levias Law Group, a family law firm on Lake Union in Seattle, with more than thirty years in practice. Before law, she was a high-risk labor and delivery nurse at University of Washington Medical Center.

She founded the Civil Survival Project, which teaches advocacy skills to people who have been in prison, after a class of them started teaching her. Camille is a lawyer, a mum and a social justice warrior too.

"My real hope is that my readers close the book and reflect on what they've learned."
